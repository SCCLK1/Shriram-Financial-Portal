Product Overview

How the Micro Digest Works

A simple overview of how the SAMC Micro Digest automatically aggregates live financial markets, formats key metrics into branded cards, and delivers them for daily client communications.

End-to-End Workflow

From market data retrieval to daily WhatsApp publishing.

1

Automated Data Fetch

When you click Generate, the system fetches live market data directly from verified financial feeds (Yahoo Finance API and Exchange feeds).

2

Data Processing

The system cleans the data, calculates daily point and percentage changes, determines market directions, and formats currency values in IST format.

3

Image Card Rendering

High-resolution PNG cards are rendered using brand-approved layout templates, corporate logos, and statutory compliance disclaimers.

4

Distribution & Archiving

Cards are ready to copy or download for WhatsApp sharing, automatically posted to Teams, and archived for up to 15 days.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time is data updated?
Data is fetched live whenever you click Generate Card. During market hours (9:15 AM to 3:30 PM IST), equity prices reflect near real-time values with typical 15-minute exchange delays.
Can we view cards generated on previous days?
Yes! Simply click the Archives button in the main application menu to view and download cards generated over the past 15 days.
How does the Microsoft Teams integration work?
Clicking Publish to Teams sends an automated payload containing the generated card image and market summary to configured Microsoft Teams channels via Webhook.
